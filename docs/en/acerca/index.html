About Neuroteca

What it is

Neuroteca is an open-access interactive atlas that presents neuroanatomy and neurophysiology together. It is written for students of medicine, neuroscience and psychology, but it assumes no prior background: the fundamentals are part of the atlas itself, as glossary entries, rather than a prerequisite.

It is a teaching resource, schematic by design. It is not a clinical or surgical reference and must not be used for diagnostic or therapeutic decisions.

How it is built

Every structure is an entry with its anatomy, its physiology, its Latin term according to the Terminologia Anatomica, its synonyms and its sources. Entries are data kept apart from the application, so the content can grow for years while the site is rewritten.

Declared granularity. Every entry states the level of detail at which it is currently represented, from overall shape (N1) to circuit (N5), and that level is always shown. A correct N1 has no nuclei inside it, and that is fine: the atlas never claims more precision than it has.

The nervous system is not a tree. A structure can belong to several divisions at once; the flocculonodular lobe, for instance, is part of both the vermis and the hemispheres. The data model reflects this with division axes instead of forcing a false hierarchy.

Citable. Every structure has a stable address by identifier, which does not change as the hierarchy evolves. The site is static, works without JavaScript and depends on no external service that could disappear.

How it is verified

No statement is published without a locatable source: work, edition and page, or DOI, or section of an open-access textbook. Every entry records who wrote it and whether it has been reviewed; only the author's review moves an entry from draft to reviewed, and readers see that status.

Visual models declare their origin, licence, level of detail and whether they are provisional or definitive. An approximate model is never presented as definitive.

Author and licences

Neuroteca is written and maintained by Maximiliano Alamilla Rodríguez (ORCID 0009-0000-7890-4569) as an independent open-access project, with the help of artificial-intelligence tools to draft text that is then verified against the sources. A tool is not a source: whatever lacks a reference is not published.

The code is free under the MIT licence. The content (entries, models and diagrams) is under CC BY-SA 4.0, except for models derived from third parties, which carry their own licence and attribution on their entry. The full repository is at github.com/neuroteca/neuroteca.github.io.

How to cite

Alamilla Rodríguez, M. Neuroteca: a living atlas of the nervous system. https://neuroteca.github.io. Licence CC BY-SA 4.0.

To cite a specific structure, use the address of its entry: it is stable and will not change. Machine-readable citation metadata is in the repository's CITATION.cff file.
